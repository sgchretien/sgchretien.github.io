01 / Research

Research
interests

My research explores mathematical and statistical aspects of machine learning, with particular interest in reliability, decision-making and the structure of complex data.

01 — Uncertainty

Statistical learning & uncertainty

Conformal prediction, distribution shifts and statistical guarantees for data-driven procedures.

02 — Sequential

Online learning & control

Sequential learning, decision theory, reinforcement learning and optimization.

03 — Geometry

Geometry, kernels & OT

Optimal transport, structured representations, spectral methods and high-dimensional data.

04 — Algorithms

Robust optimization

Stochastic methods, robust estimation and reliable computational learning.
