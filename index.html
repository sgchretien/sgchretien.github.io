01 / Research

Research
interests

My research explores mathematical methods for learning and optimization, spanning statistical estimation, structured reconstruction, dynamical systems, and uncertainty quantification.

01 — Estimation

EM and Proximal Algorithms

Expectation–maximization, proximal methods, and convergence analysis.

02 — Inverse Problems

Sparse and Low-Rank Recovery

Sparse signal recovery, low-rank matrix and tensor reconstruction, and inverse problems.

03 — Path Representations

Signature Theory

Path signatures, algebraic structures, and representations of sequential data.

04 — Operations Research

Combinatorial Optimization and Vehicle Routing

Discrete optimization, combinatorial algorithms, and vehicle routing problems (VRP).

05 — Dynamics

Dynamical Systems and Koopman Operators

Nonlinear dynamical systems, Koopman operators, and spectral representations.

06 — Statistical Guarantees

Uncertainty Quantification and Conformal Prediction

Distribution-free predictive inference, prediction sets, and coverage guarantees.
